## Notes

1. **Recovery > 100% is possible** — frozen_β seed42 from10k (106.1%) and CDL teacher both outperform ori-L2R ordering at 50k. The learned orders can be better than pure L2R for the model's loss.
2. **Both seeds consistent** — seed-123 (L0H2, 86.0% recovery) and seed-42 (L0H4, 106.1%) both strongly positive. Recovery > 100% = method beats L2R reference.
3. **CDL teacher is cross-seed** — CDL teacher uses seed=2, not seed=123 (the frozen_beta "seed2" group). Treat as supplementary evidence.
4. **from40k results are weak across all methods** — the model is near convergence, leaving little room for order optimization.
5. All numbers use `val_ori_l2r_block` (model evaluated with L2R-ordered blocks), NOT training objective.
